# Demo script · about 6 minutes

1. **Start and scope (45 s).** Open the app and show the drifting question chips: hover stops a row, and clicking a chip types its question into the bar. Open Sources. Explain why the prototype covers only C16QS and C20QM.
2. **Sourced facts (60 s).** Ask for C16QS peak speeds and India LTE bands. Open each cited product page and find Technical Data.
3. **Product discovery (60 s).** Use the published peak 20 Mbps downlink question. Show both figures and why C20QM clears the threshold. Keep the peak-performance qualifier visible.
4. **Unsupported and feedback (75 s).** Ask about a custom antenna. Show the support link. Select Needs review and Weak citation on a cited answer, then show the matching entry in Supabase for the connected Worker, or in the local preview's feedback folder.
5. **Architecture and controls (90 s).** Walk through the diagram, Supabase embedding count, exact search, Gemini evidence IDs, Worker secrets, and the Worker-reported timings in the verification record.
6. **Website handoff (30 s).** Explain the widget and API integration, who owns product evidence and what needs sign-off before a company rollout.
